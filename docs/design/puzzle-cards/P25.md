The rack, left to right, in fixed slots (not grouped, not true-first): `position_b`, `trace_a`,
`identity`, `trace`, `identity_b`, `trace_b`, `position`. Every lift reads the card aloud; a card
in a frame shows its whole face. Saves keep each frame's card; unknown card ids are dropped on load.

The eight staff indicators are closed. The witness terminal is open. The procedure is `witness_test`.

## Solution

**Movement trace → BODY**, **chamber position → PLACE**, **contract identity → NAME**. Routing switch to **WITNESS**. **ROUTE** (commit).

## Wrong answers

Routing to COPY: a preview strip prints "WOULD FINALIZE OBSERVER" and ROUTE refuses until the switch is back on WITNESS. No surprise ending, no lockout.

A wrong card of the right category (a decoy in its own frame): the moment it is seated, that frame's plate lights "DOES NOT CORRESPOND TO OBSERVER" and stays lit while the card is there (it is derived from the frames, so it survives a save). Nothing locks: the card lifts back out, or another card displaces it. ROUTE with a lit plate refuses: "ROUTE won't turn. The BODY plate reads: DOES NOT CORRESPOND TO OBSERVER." Evidence `plate_mismatch`.

A card of the wrong category: ROUTE refuses and the frame names its category, as before ("ROUTE won't turn. The BODY plate lights: 'What the subject did, in the order it was done.'").
