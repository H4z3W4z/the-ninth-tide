Show, don't print: there are no consequence cards, no printed outcomes and no CONFIRM button. The player learns each route by wiring it as Venn described it, then watching it.

## Solution

A dry run of each route, either order. Each cord is moved from socket to socket by hand (lift it at its contact, drop it in a socket: the sim action `patch:<cord>:<socket>`; its own contact again puts it back in its own line).

- **SEVER:** FEED A, FEED B and E.V. into OPEN, then PREVIEW SEVER.
- **CONTINUE:** E.V. into A.M. and A.M. into TERMINAL, then PREVIEW CONTINUE.

Cords a route does not name may be anywhere. Each correct preview runs on the plate's lamps, then the window glance shows it. When both routes have been rehearsed on a correct patch, P26 completes (commit) and the checkpoint is taken.

## Wrong answers

A PREVIEW on a patch that is not its route's: the plate lights ROUTE INCOMPLETE (steady, until the patch changes) and she says "Not like that. He said what each one does." Nothing is consumed, nothing is previewed, no glance, no rehearsal recorded. A.M. into itself is refused ("Not into itself."). A repeated correct preview only plays it again.

## Outputs

Pre-ending checkpoint save (named; used by "Return to final decision"), taken only at that commit. The patch (`state.patch`, each cord's socket) is saved and validated on load (unknown cords or sockets, and A.M. into itself, go back to their own line).
