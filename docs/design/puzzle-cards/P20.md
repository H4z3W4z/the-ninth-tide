## Reveal and dread

The sealed personal paragraph of the letter opens: "You have already refused to make a person disappear for the convenience of a form." Ada, angry: "You could have asked." Then: "You knew I would finish the work before I understood what it was." The funnel is in another room, but the office answers anyway, very quietly, from the oilskin on hook 7: "Yes." A pause. "I was afraid you would be kinder than me. I was also counting on it."

Slice weave: the drawer photograph is now pinned to the board by itself (Ada did not pin it). Its back, readable at last, in Venn's hand: "Not mine. It was in the developing tray on the 15th. She will need to see herself from outside. — E.V."
