## Solution

## Wrong answers

- Pressure not 2: "PRESSURE NOT EQUAL" with the gauge needle off the marked 2; the gate stays shut.
- `[ 2` in either slot, or a mismatched pair: "NO CORRESPONDING LANDING." The false third landing is visible through the gate as a landing that ends in a wall of water; it is never a random doorway.
- Plates swapped (`] 1` upper): "Brackets face the wrong way across the boundary."
