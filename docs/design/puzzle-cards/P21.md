# P21. The Station's Real Plan

**Room:** R03 Chart Room, view A. Close-up `c_plan`: the station plan (1889, revised E.V. 1927) under a glass lid, drawn with **north to the right** and its own registration cross marked LIGHTHOUSE printed beside the lantern gallery. Rooms drawn: lantern gallery over chart room, listening room and wet laboratory side by side with the dry stair between them, the observation chamber outlined and marked WITNESS, office and records vault side by side below, kitchen and bunks in the margin as PERSONAL EVIDENCE. Beside the plan: a slot for his composite chart, two knobs that turn it, three register pins (his LIGHTHOUSE, BUOY, DATUM STONE), a brass CLIP, and a brass WITNESS pin in its slot under the lid.

**Tide:** VII, closes it. Target 4 minutes.

## Requires

`p19_complete` and `p20_complete` (the lid's screws are out), and the `composite_chart` (P03) in the notebook: it is the thing laid on the plan.

## Solution

It is P03's mechanic again, on the plan:

1. **plan_lay**: lay his composite over the plan. It comes off the table as it was clipped there, north up.
2. **plan_rotate_cw** once (**plan_rotate** is the same; **plan_rotate_ccw** turns the other way): his north arrow now points the way the plan's does.
3. **plan_snap_lighthouse**: pin his lighthouse cross on the plan's lighthouse cross (or click his cross on the sheet).
4. **plan_clip** (or her `chart_clips` used on the clip). Registered: the curve runs down through the lantern gallery, the chart room, the listening room and the laboratory, and those rooms fill with a seated body outline (`assets/stages/R03/p21_body_overlay.png`, flat navy contour, matte sea-glass fill). The office and the vault lie outside it, underneath, as NAME. His three marks end in the chamber in the middle.
5. **plan_pin**, then **plan_zone_centre**: the WITNESS pin goes where his three marks end, the centre chamber (commit).

## Wrong answers

- Any other registration (north up, another quarter, his buoy or datum on the plan's lighthouse, unpinned) at the clip: "The line runs through walls. Not like that." Nothing is consumed; the composite stays as she left it.
- The WITNESS pin on another zone stays in the hand: laboratory/listening "Not there. That's the body.", chart/lantern "Not there. That's where it's looking from.", office/vault "That's the name, underneath it. Not the witness.", the margin "Those are the people, not the instrument."
- The pin before the registration: "A brass pin, stamped WITNESS. I don't know where it goes. Not yet."

## Reveal and dread

The tracing goes clear and the line stays on the plan. The rooms it touched fill in, all one colour, into a figure sitting with its hands on its knees; the chamber where its heart would be stays hollow. When the pin goes in, the witness frame prints, in the recorder's type: ADA MERCER.
