# P21. The Station's Real Plan

**Room:** R03 Chart Room, view A. Close-up `c_plan` (the station plan with four label tiles BODY, PLACE, NAME, WITNESS and four target zones: laboratory/listening pair, chart/lantern pair, office/vault pair, the centre chamber outline marked WITNESS; kitchen and bunks printed in the margin as PERSONAL EVIDENCE).

**Tide:** VII, closes it. Target 4 minutes.

## Requires

`p19_complete`, `p20_complete`.

## Evidence

| Evidence | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Listening and breathing traces are BODY | Apparatus labels: R07 frame label, R08 nameplate (`reference_respiration`), R04 mixer. `index_protocol`. |
| Surveyed bearings are PLACE | R03 composite chart, R09 bearing strip (`nora_bearing`). |
| Registers are NAME | R02 contract and register, R10 folders. |
| The chamber outline is WITNESS | Printed on the plan. |

## Solution

**BODY → laboratory/listening**, **PLACE → chart/lantern**, **NAME → office/vault**, **WITNESS → centre**. Press the plan's **FIX** pin (commit).

## Wrong answers

A misplaced tile shows the instrument references it summarises beside the zone it is on: "PLACE: bearings, charts. This zone holds the balance and the funnels." Kitchen and bunks accept no tile: "Those are the people, not the instrument."
