# P18. The Director's Perfect Record

**Room:** R10 Records Vault, view B. Close-up `c_folders` (three folders BODY, PLACE, NAME with cover examples, a comparison tray printed TRAINING SUBJECT, a session-date dial, a CHAIR dial over a plan of the 1927 session table, COMPARE key, printed TRAINING RELEASE panel).

**Tide:** VI. Parallel with P17. Target 5 minutes.

## Requires

`p16_complete`. Items `nora_frame`, `nora_spatial_strip`, `nora_register_card`, `contact_sheet`.

## The deduction

Filing is not the puzzle. Her three records are laid on the tray (one item use: any of the three, on the tray or on any folder) and **file themselves** as the training subject, each into its own folder. No category is chosen. The strip prints `TRAINING SUBJECT: BELL, N.`

The puzzle is **which chair was hers** at the 1927 session, and on which date.

| Rook's protocol card: "A subject is complete when BODY, PLACE and NAME correspond in one chair on one session date." | Inside the cabinet lid (`protocol_card`). |

## Solution

Lay any of her records on the tray (all three file themselves). Set the CHAIR dial to **6** and the date dial to **18 Oct 1927**. **COMPARE** (commit).

## Wrong answers

Chair and date are read separately and both reported on the strip; her records stay filed.

- Wrong chair: "CHAIR DOES NOT CORRESPOND." Ada: "Not her chair."
- Wrong date: "DATE DOES NOT CORRESPOND." Ada: "Not the session date." On 18 Oct 1931 the strip adds "NOT YET." (`not_yet`).
- Nothing on the tray: "NO TRAINING SUBJECT". Before P16: "NO SESSION ON RECORD".
- Laying a record without holding all three: "Her records go together. I haven't got all three." Nothing is taken.

Nothing is consumed. The release, any folder, leaving the close-up and a load all return her records.
