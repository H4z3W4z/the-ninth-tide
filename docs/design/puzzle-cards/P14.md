## Solution

## Wrong answers

It is not a story line; nothing is consumed and no section, tab or strip moves. Which slot is wrong is never marked: the player holds the print against the card in the notebook. Moving any section clears the print. Before her card has been seen, SEAL says only "I need her trace to compare. It's on the nail by the range." Empty slots are outlined (dashed). No breath control, pitch or hold-to-press is ever involved.
