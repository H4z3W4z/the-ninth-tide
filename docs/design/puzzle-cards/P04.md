**Tide:** II. Target 3 minutes.

Ada: "The zero's out." / "The interval's out." / "Both out." Nothing advances on a wrong pull; no randomness.

- Evidence `stair_threshold`: "The observation hatch opens onto a dry stair going down. The window beside it is full of seawater at the same level."
