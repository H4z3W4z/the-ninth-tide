A compound puzzle (GDD rule: compound tasks count as one). Part 1 is the slice's drawer in R02; part 2 is the registration in R03. The drawer supplies the second chart sheet the registration needs.

**Room:** Part 1: R02 view A, close-up `c_drawer`. Part 2: R03 view A, close-up `c_registration`.

## Solution

**Part 1, the drawer (slice, unchanged).** 06:31 + 12 h 21 m = 18:52. Set dials **1-8-5-2**, try the drawer. It holds Venn's watch stopped at 18:52, the photograph of this window from outside, his folded tide sheet for 17 Oct, and his inspection lens.

**Part 2, the registration.** In `c_registration`: choose the **17 Oct** table sheet, lay Venn's 17 Oct sheet over it, rotate Venn's sheet so **north points up**, snap **lighthouse cross to lighthouse cross**, then **clip** (commit). The composite curve crosses interval band **3** and has the shape of the three scratches on the R03 observation hatch.
