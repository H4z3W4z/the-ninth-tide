# P02. Tomorrow's Recording

**Room:** R04 Listening Room, view A. Close-up `c_spindle` (cylinder spindle with three seats, four bands in a felt tray, transcript card, needle arm).

**Tide:** I. Parallel with P03. Target 4-5 minutes.

## Requires

`p01_complete`.

## Evidence

| Evidence | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Four wax bands labelled B, D, A, C (in that order in the tray, deliberately not alphabetical). Band C has a START notch; band D also has a START notch and **14 OCT** scratched on its rim; band A's diagonal seam runs off both edges; band B's seam stops at an END notch. | Felt tray, `c_spindle`. |
| The transcript card above the spindle is headed **17 OCT**. This is the tell: only C belongs to that day. | Transcript card, `c_spindle`. |
| The spindle's marked start end (an engraved arrow and the word START) on the left. | Spindle, `c_spindle`. |
| Each band edge has three snap positions, engraved I, II, III; the seam is drawn so it meets the next band only at one position. | Band edges. |
| "Listening Room: preserve the recording before winding again." | R02 note under the duty card (`note`). |

## Solution

Deduce that D is the wrong day (14 OCT against the card's 17 OCT). Place **C → A → B** from the spindle's start end. Rotate to snap positions **C: I, A: III, B: II**, so the diagonal seam runs unbroken from START to END. Lower the needle (**PLAY**, the commit).

## Wrong answers

- Wrong order: on PLAY, the joint where the seam breaks is highlighted on the trace and the transcript shows a broken line ("— seam —"), with Ada: "It jumps at the second join. The seam doesn't carry on." Order is never named. Nothing is damaged.
- Right order, wrong rotation: the same, naming which joint ("first join" or "second join").
- START notch not at the start end: "The start notch is at the wrong end."
- The decoy: **D → A → B** at **III, I, III** also carries the seam unbroken. PLAY prints "VENN: Greywake, fourteenth October..." and Ada says "That's the wrong day." (both held story lines); the date on D's rim and on the card are ringed. Nothing is gained or consumed. If D starts the spindle and the seam breaks at the second join, the broken line already reads "VENN: Greywake, fourteenth Oc—".

## Outputs

- Flag `p02_complete`. Move `recording_played`.
- Evidence `recording_tomorrow` (full transcript and trace image), `calibration_zero` ("Calibration zero: 4. Venn's voice, on the recording.").
- The recording becomes replayable from `c_spindle` and from the notebook (transcript with speaker labels).

## Consumed

Nothing. The bands stay on the spindle.

## Transcript (Venn, recorded; speaker-labelled)

> VENN: Greywake, seventeenth October. Calibration zero is four. Write it down, whoever you are.
> VENN: The recorder is accurate. That is the difficulty.
> [a chair creaks on the cylinder. Then, close to the horn, someone who is not Venn breathes in.]
> VENN: Eighteenth October. Mercer will be standing at the chart table.

The final line is dated tomorrow. Ada: "Mercer. He had my name before I signed anything." Then, checking: "It's on the contract. He wrote the contract." (Her name is on the visible contract from the start; nothing implies surveillance of the player.)
