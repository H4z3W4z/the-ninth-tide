# P01. A Little More Light

**Room:** R02 Intake Office, view A. Close-up `c_repair` (lamp, tray, maintenance plate). Opened from `lamp` or `tray`.

**Tide:** I. Target 2-3 minutes. Teaches inspect, select item, use item on socket, commit, consequence.

## Requires

New game (`started`). The duty card and closure contract are readable from the start (`duty`, `contract`).

## Evidence

| Evidence | Where it physically is |
| --- | --- |
| Repair card: "1. Set supply to OFF. 2. Fit straight bridge B. 3. Set supply to ON." | Maintenance plate inside the lamp, `c_repair` (approved `repair_panel` art). |
| Reference shape: straight B; "Curved spare C will not seat." | Same plate, reference sketch. |
| Straight bridge B and curved spare C, labelled | Maintenance tray, `c_repair`. |
| "Begin with the desk lamp. The spare bridge is in the tray." | Duty card, `c_duty` (`duty`). |
| Venn's log row: "Spare B left in the tray for her." | Readable only after P01 (not needed to solve). |

## Solution (unchanged from the slice and AGENTS.md)

1. Supply switch **OFF**.
2. Select **bridge B**, then the contact terminals: B seats.
3. Supply switch **ON**. Commit happens on this switch.

## Wrong answers

| Attempt | Result | Consumes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fit any part while supply is ON | Spark (`spark`, `sudden`), "Ah! It bit me. Supply off first, Mercer." | Nothing |
| Fit curved C (supply OFF) | Clink, "The curve won't lie flat across both terminals." C stays in the tray. | Nothing |
| Fit with nothing selected | "Pick a spare from the tray first." | Nothing |
| Supply ON with no bridge | "Supply on. Nothing. The circuit's still open." | Nothing |

## Outputs

- Flag `p01_complete`. R02-local `bridge_installed`, `supply_on`.
- Evidence `lamp` ("Desk lamp repaired: supply off, straight bridge B, supply on.").
- Move `lamp_mended`.
- Opens `strip_upper` (R03, R04): "And somewhere down the corridor, two latches let go."
- Unlocks R02 note under the duty card (`note`), the readable tide log, and O01.

## Consumed

`bridge_b` (installed; notebook keeps the installed record). `spare_c` is never consumed.

## Hints

2. "The plate inside the lamp says what kind of contact it takes, and what to do before fitting one."
3. "Open the lamp. Switch the supply OFF, select straight bridge B, tap the terminals, then switch ON."

## Notebook

My initials, gone dull as if written days ago. Also: register both chart sheets at the lighthouse cross; preserve the recording before winding again."

## Reveal and dread

Mechanical first: switch click, light (0.35 s), then the far latches (1.3 s). The wrongness is quiet and comes after: the chair's shadow falls toward the lamp (`shadow`), and under the duty card her own initials sign off a job she has just done, the graphite days old. The window now shows the lamp more than the sea.
