## Solution

Mount Nora's frame. Aim at **STATION 3**. Use the **lens** on the reflected bowl to confirm the **square patch** ("A square patch, riveted. A bowl, at the cross."; the only line that confirms, and the frame's relation mark lights). **RECORD BEARING** (commit). After the commit the selector is locked: "The bearing's recorded. I leave it where it is."

## Wrong answers

On STATION 3 the rotating miss line includes the contract rule, "Do not certify an observation you have not made."

## Reveal and dread

The bearing strip feeds out (mechanical). Then the horizon stands up: the sea outside the glass becomes a plane standing vertically, carrying the reflections of the rooms behind Ada, the office lamp a warm point among them. A slow line passes across the whole surface like a lid opening, once (`motion`; still: line drawn mid-pass). It does not close again. No creature is ever shown.
