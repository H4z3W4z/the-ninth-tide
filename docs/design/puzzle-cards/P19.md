# P19. Speak to the Empty Channel

**Room:** R04 Listening Room, view B. Close-up `c_channels` (MOTOR switch, channels A, B, C open/closed, WITNESS key, socket marks: strokes I, II, III cut into the brass over A, B, C, no words; printed procedure).

**Tide:** VII. Parallel with P20. Target 4 minutes.

## Requires

`p17_complete`, `p18_complete`.

## Evidence

| Evidence | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The dead channel is Venn's station | `witness_procedure` (P17). |
| Venn's seal has three cuts | `venn_seal` (P08). |
| Socket marks: I, II, III cut into the brass over A, B, C (no words; the seal rubbing is the key). | `c_channels` (`socket_cuts`). |
| Procedure: "Address a witness on a quiet line." It says what, never how. | `c_channels` (`procedure`). |
| What makes a line not quiet is taught by failure (below). | `line_occupied`, `line_crowded`. |

## Solution

**MOTOR off**, close **A** and **B**, open **C**, press **WITNESS** (commit). Then ask the three fixed questions (all three are required evidence; any order).

## Wrong answers

Taught by failure; nothing breaks and nothing is consumed. Once P17 and P18 are done:

- WITNESS with the motor on: the cylinder is still turning, so channel C plays Rook's 1927 dictation (ROOK (channel C, 1927): "Session of the eighteenth of October. Eight present." / "The instruments are recording. Witness station C is vacant."), then Ada: "The line is occupied." Status tape LINE OCCUPIED. Notebook `line_occupied`.
- WITNESS with A or B open (motor off): the P17 session voices bleed onto C (the overlapped VENN / NORA fragments), then Ada: "Two other voices on the line. His can't get through them." Status tape LINE CROWDED. Notebook `line_crowded`.
- With C closed: the key clicks and nothing answers.

Before P17 and P18 every WITNESS press only clicks: nothing answers.

## The exchange (speaker-labelled; replayable)

- **"Where are they?"** VENN: "Here. In the instruments. I kept them where they could still be reached. I never found how to let them out."
- **"Are they still themselves?"** VENN: "I don't know. I can't promise you they're whole. They answer. That's all I can tell you."
- **"What do you need from me?"** VENN: "If you break it, they end, every trace. If you don't, someone has to stay. Physically. Here. A witness, not a signature. I've done it three days and I'm wearing out."
