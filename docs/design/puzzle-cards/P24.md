# P24. Leave the Last Space Open

**Room:** R11 Dry Stair, view 2. Close-up `c_chamber_door` (three evidence frames BODY, PLACE, NAME; a witness socket; an automatic COPY port; OPEN ACCESS lever).

**Simplified (p2):** BODY and PLACE come pre-seated with Nora's trace (`nora_frame`) and bearing (`nora_spatial_strip`). The first time the live door's close-up opens they are taken from the inventory if held, or simply drawn seated if not, and Ada says: "Her trace and her bearing are already in the frames. The folders sent them down." Seated frames do not come out ("The clips won't give.") and refuse other records. The puzzle is two choices: which NAME card, and where the witness bridge goes.

**Tide:** VIII, closes it. Target 4 minutes.

## Requires

`p22_complete`, `p23_complete`. Items `amended_nora_card`, `witness_bridge` (and `nora_register_card`, which the COPY port pushes out if she has none). `nora_frame` and `nora_spatial_strip` are seated for her.

## Solution

BODY and PLACE are already seated. Choose **`amended_nora_card` → NAME** (not the original the COPY port pushed out). Fit **`witness_bridge` → WITNESS**, not COPY. Leave **COPY bare**. Pull **OPEN ACCESS** (commit).

## Wrong answers

The original card in NAME: "RECORD CLOSED. ACCESS REFUSED. A shutter drops over her name." (reversible: the shutter lifts when the card comes out). Anything in COPY, the bridge included: "WOULD FINALIZE SUBJECT. The lever won't move while anything's in COPY." NAME empty: "RECORD INCOMPLETE: NAME". Bridge nowhere: "NO WITNESS. RECORD WILL CLOSE." Nothing consumed.
