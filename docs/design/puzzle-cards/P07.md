## Solution

The eight initialled bowls set themselves. The player places only the unsigned square-patch bowl, on the **blank place by the door**. Turn the tally wheel to **9** (nine notches up, or four back from 0). Pull **COMPARE** (commit): it checks wheel == 9 together with the bowl placement.

## Wrong answers
