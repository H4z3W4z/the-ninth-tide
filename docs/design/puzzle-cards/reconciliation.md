# Reconciliation: the Intake Office slice and GDD v1.1

The slice (rounds 1-3) invented beats the GDD never had, and compressed a whole game's escalation into one room's Tide I. This file records what each beat became, and every place the cards depart from GDD v1.1. The rule used: keep a slice beat in Tide I if it is a discrepancy, a puzzle or one impossible view (GDD 11 horror curve, Tides I-II); move it later if it is an escalation the rest of the game needs to earn.

## 3. New canon the cards add (the GDD left it open)

- **Closure contract text** (new asset, under the duty card in `c_duty`; evidence `contract`): "GREYWAKE SURVEY STATION. CONTRACT OF CLOSURE. Addressed to: Miss Ada Mercer, cataloguer. Inventory instruments. Reconcile outstanding records. Restore departure signal. Do not certify an observation you have not made. Collection launch (silhouette; pennant, two stripes) answers the departure signal; scheduled 18 Oct, morning high water." Signed for the Board, countersigned E. Venn.
- **The eight indexed staff** and their initials (README roster). Only Nora Bell, Rook, Ives and Venn were named in the GDD.
- **Dates:** Index session 18 Oct 1927, exactly four years before the collection; the Morrow Harbour Board inquiry 1929; missing dockworker Daniel Roe.
- **Movement whitelist** for P05 and P25 (`flags.md` 3).
- **Exact arrangements** the GDD left as "align" or "snap": P02 rotations I-III-II; P03 dates and crosses; P12 plates `[ 1` / `] 1` and the false `[ 2`; P13 samples N, W, F; P16 clock and lamp times; P17 offset -2; P19 Venn's three answers.
- **Lines added on the contract rule:** P06 refuses to ring before the lens look, P15 refuses before the lens confirms the patch. Both quote "Do not certify an observation you have not made", which makes the lens matter and rhymes with Ada's backstory.

## 5. Things the room teams must not do

- Do not let the T1X log rewrite fire from the drawer alone (the slice rule); it needs `tide_done(1)`.
- Do not let the threshold's "turn round" end or branch the game.
- Do not apply more than one look-away change per return, or apply any before its band.
- Do not reuse slice plates out of their mapped bands without the art lead checking the captures.
