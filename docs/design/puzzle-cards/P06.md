## Solution

Use the **lens** on the boat's window (writes `launch_mismatch`). On the mooring panel set **UNVERIFIED RETURN**, ring the bell under the **broken pennant** (the second bell, action `bell_2`; commit). The broken pennant is the same glyph as on the UNVERIFIED tally, so the match is seen, not counted.

## Wrong answers

- VERIFIED: "The pennant's wrong. The contract boat has two stripes. This one has three." Panel resets.
- Any other bell: "That's not the bell for a mismatched vessel. Each bell hangs under its own pennant."
- UNVERIFIED and the broken pennant's bell before using the lens on the window: refused, quoting the contract: "I haven't looked at it properly. 'Do not certify an observation you have not made.'" The panel stays set; the lens look then completes it on the next ring.
- Gangway, once: one deliberate loop. Ada steps onto the gangway and the view cuts to her stepping onto the same jetty from the shed side. "I've just walked onto the jetty I was standing on." Recorded (`gangway_loop` R01-local); further gangway clicks: "Once was enough." No progress lost.
