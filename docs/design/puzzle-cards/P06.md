# P06. The Boat That Came Back

**Room:** R01 Jetty. Close-ups `c_boat_window` (lens view), `c_mooring_panel`.

**Tide:** II, closes it. Target 4-5 minutes.

## Requires

| Evidence | Where |

| The arriving boat: **three-stripe** pennant, motionless in moving water. | R01 base view. |

| Mooring panel: VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED RETURN tally; station bells 1-4; placard "Mismatched vessel: set UNVERIFIED RETURN. Ring station bell 2. Do not board." | `c_mooring_panel`. |

## Solution

Use the **lens** on the boat's window (writes `launch_mismatch`). On the mooring panel set **UNVERIFIED RETURN**, ring **bell 2** (commit).

## Wrong answers

- VERIFIED: "The pennant's wrong. The contract boat has two stripes. This one has three." Panel resets.
- Any other bell: "That's not the bell for a mismatched vessel. The placard says two."
- UNVERIFIED and bell 2 before using the lens on the window: refused, quoting the contract: "I haven't looked at it properly. 'Do not certify an observation you have not made.'" The panel stays set; the lens look then completes it on the next ring.
- Gangway, once: one deliberate loop. Ada steps onto the gangway and the view cuts to her stepping onto the same jetty from the shed side. "I've just walked onto the jetty I was standing on." Recorded (`gangway_loop` R01-local); further gangway clicks: "Once was enough." No progress lost.

## Reveal and dread

Bell 2's note is answered by nothing. The boat withdraws without turning: it slides backward, stern first, with its bow toward the station the whole way (`motion`; still: two poses). Through the lens before it goes, the chart room in its window has someone standing at the chart table. That is the P02 prediction kept, seen from outside. Then the capsule rises up the mooring line on its own.
