Free experiment (teaching by failure): INPUT → COPY then TEST AMENDMENT types "AMENDMENT 1 APPENDED. RECORD CLOSED." on the strip and stamps CLOSED; every drawer in the catalogue knocks at once (sudden `staff_slam`), the sea-glass stripe running up from STAFF stops, and the frame photographs on the shelves wall go blank for one beat, all sim events. RESET reopens the record and starts the stripe again instantly. It never sets `p23_complete`. Nothing is locked or lost.
