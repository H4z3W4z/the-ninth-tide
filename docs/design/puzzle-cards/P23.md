# P23. The Signature That Must Not Arrive

**Room:** R10 Records Vault, view B. Close-up `c_model` (the Index model: INPUT, COPY, WITNESS terminals, a lead with two plugs, TEST AMENDMENT key, RESET key, a faceplate, a removable witness bridge seated across WITNESS).

**Tide:** VIII. Parallel with P22. Target 4 minutes.

## Solution

Connect **INPUT → WITNESS**, leave **COPY disconnected**, press **TEST AMENDMENT** (commit). The model accepts a new line and stays OPEN. The bridge comes free.

Free experiment (teaching by failure): INPUT → COPY then TEST AMENDMENT types "AMENDMENT 1 APPENDED. RECORD CLOSED." on the strip and stamps CLOSED; every STAFF drawer slams shut together (sudden `staff_slam`) and the frame photographs on the shelves wall go blank for one beat, both sim events. RESET reopens the record and STAFF instantly. It never sets `p23_complete`. Nothing is locked or lost.

## Wrong answers

COPY connected (alone or with WITNESS): "CLOSED" stamped on the model's strip; Ada: "The copy answered for her. That's what it did to them." RESET restores. Nothing connected: "No input."
