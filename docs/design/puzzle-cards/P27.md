# P27. Sign, or Break the Line

**Room:** R12 Observation Chamber, view C. Close-up `c_final` (feed contacts with the yoke seat; Ada's terminal with the bridge seat; preview lamps; two confirmation buttons with their full text).

**Tide:** IX, closes it. Target 3 minutes plus ending.

## Requires

`p26_complete`. For CONTINUE: item `witness_bridge`. For SEVER: the severance yoke in the R12 yoke tray (room-local, always present).

## Solution

**SEVER:** fit the **severance yoke** across the two labelled feed contacts; PREVIEW (the eight lines extinguish in the preview only); confirm **BREAK CONNECTION AND LEAVE**. Ada pulls the handle.

**CONTINUE:** seat the **witness bridge** in Ada's terminal; PREVIEW (the active line moves from Venn to Ada); confirm **REMAIN AS WITNESS**. Ada writes the first new amendment.

Until a confirmation is pressed, the yoke and bridge can be moved freely between configurations. Each confirmation needs its preview first.

## Wrong answers

None punitive. Confirm without preview: the button reads "Preview first." Yoke and bridge both fitted: "One or the other."

## Outputs

## Consumed

## Ending A: The Unrecorded Shore (SEVER)

The instruments fall silent in sequence, room by room, with pauses long enough to hear what they carried: R04 funnels, R08 breath, R07 jars level, R05 spoon settles, R06 indentations rise, R03 pen lifts. Venn, once: "All right." The outside surface recedes into ordinary water. Ada walks through the office and takes the corrected record. On the jetty the real launch arrives with the two-stripe pennant; its window shows its own empty cabin. Harbour inquiry: asked to certify the station empty, she writes eight names and Venn's, and: "I cannot certify their absence." Final image: the corrected page drying in daylight; a small water stain with no reflection.

## Ending B: The Last Observer (CONTINUE)

Venn's funnel falls silent. In the office the wet coat collapses into an ordinary garment. Ada sits. The Index offers its first automatic sentence: "SUBJECT BELL POSSESSES NO SURVIVING PERSONAL EFFECTS." She adds the correction about the repaired cup; in the kitchen, its handle warms. Another line. Another correction. Counter output: "DISCREPANCY DETECTED: SUBJECT CONTINUES." Morning reaches the windows; the station does not return to the shore. Final wide view: the lit kitchen, one small warm rectangle far inside the enormous dark surface among innumerable unlit ones. The recorder prints "LOCAL SURVEY: ONE OF..." and Ada covers it with a sheet of her own paper. "Nora Bell took her tea without sugar. Begin there."
