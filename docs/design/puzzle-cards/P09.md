**Room:** R05 Kitchen and Mess, view B. Close-up `c_service_board` (shift board with the official eight names, a token socket in two halves, an amendment slot with letter tiles, a three-position seal selector: one, two, three cuts).

## Solution

Fit **both token halves**. Leave the official **8** untouched. In the amendment slot compose **+1 / VENN / present**. Set the seal selector to **three cuts** and **stamp** (commit).

## Wrong answers

- Changing the 8 (the tiles let her try): "REJECTED: UNVERIFIABLE CORRECTION. ORIGINAL ENTRY MUST STAND." The 8 resets.
- Wrong name: "The amendment has to name the person. Nora's note names him."
- Wrong seal: "One cut is Bell's kitchen stamp. Two is the director's. Venn's has three." (Both other seal patterns are printed on the selector.)
- One token half: the socket will not turn.
