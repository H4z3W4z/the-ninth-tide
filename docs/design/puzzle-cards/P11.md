# P11. Something Still Breathing

**Room:** R08 Machine Room. Close-up `c_valves` (four toggles carrying only stamped numbers, No. 07, No. 12, No. 31, No. 44; PUMP lever; a PREVIEW key that lights the active circuit on the drawing).

**Tide:** IV. Parallel with P10. Target 4 minutes.

## Requires

`p09_complete`.

## Evidence

| Evidence | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance drawing MAINT. DWG. 4, topology only, no line names: the pump, four valve symbols numbered 07, 12, 31, 44, a STAIR GATE box, and a capped stub marked "SEALED. NEVER FEED." The loop runs pump, 07, 12, stair gate, 31, pump; the stub branches off before the gate through 44, in a later ink. | Above the valve board. |
| Toggles stamped No. 07, No. 12, No. 31, No. 44; 44 left OPEN, its seal wire cut. | Valve board. |
| Venn's repair tags give number to name (No. 07 SUPPLY, No. 12 GATE, No. 31 RETURN, No. 44 INDEX, "Do not open."). Corroboration, not required. | Ring behind the middle belt (`c_tags`). |

## Solution

**07 (SUPPLY) open, 12 (GATE) open, 31 (RETURN) open, 44 (INDEX) shut.** Engage **PUMP** (commit).

## Wrong answers

PREVIEW (and every PUMP attempt) traces the active circuit on the drawing in a dashed overlay and names the fault: "NO PRESSURE" (07 shut), "DIVERTED TO SEALED STUB" (07 and 44 open; the pump refuses to engage and Ada: "It would feed the sealed line."), "DEAD END BEFORE GATE" (12 shut), "DEAD END AT GATE" (31 shut). The fault window is printed on the drawing, so it never names a line either. No burst pipe, loss or danger.

## Outputs

- Flag `p11_complete`. Evidence `reference_respiration`.
- O04 available.

## Consumed

Nothing.

## Hints

Four tiers; the core asks "Do you want me to just say it?" before the fourth. Every tier is distinct (tiers 2 and 3 above all), and none names a line.

1. "The drawing above the valve board is the circuit: a pump, four numbered valves, the stair gate, and one capped stub."
2. "The capped stub on the drawing is the line that must never be fed. Find its valve."
3. "On the drawing, 07, 12 and 31 carry the loop out to the stair gate and back. The capped stub hangs off 44, and Venn's tag for 44 says 'Do not open.'"
4. "07, 12 and 31 open, 44 shut. Then the pump."

## Notebook

`reference_respiration`: "With the gate loop running, one belt stopped. The diaphragm kept breathing without it. Under the belt, a nameplate: REFERENCE RESPIRATION."

## Reveal and dread

The pump takes up with a heavy, satisfying thump and the gate gauge (seen later at R11) reads powered. Then one belt winds down and stops. The diaphragm does not. Its next inhalation pulls fine dust horizontally across the floor toward it (`motion`; still: dust streaks). The plate under the belt reads REFERENCE RESPIRATION.

## Room states after

R08 P11-done.

## Tests

All 16 toggle states; 44 (INDEX) open never engages; each fault name correct; repeated PUMP idempotent; the drawing, its notebook entries, its fault window and the hints never name SUPPLY, RETURN or INDEX; the hint ladder is the room's own four tiers with the core's confirmation before the last.
