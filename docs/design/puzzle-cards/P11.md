## Solution

**07 (SUPPLY) open, 12 (GATE) open, 31 (RETURN) open, 44 (INDEX) shut.** Engage **PUMP** (commit).

## Wrong answers

PREVIEW (and every PUMP attempt) traces the active circuit on the drawing in a dashed overlay and names the fault: "NO PRESSURE" (07 shut), "DIVERTED TO SEALED STUB" (07 and 44 open; the pump refuses to engage and Ada: "It would feed the sealed line."), "DEAD END BEFORE GATE" (12 shut), "DEAD END AT GATE" (31 shut). The fault window is printed on the drawing, so it never names a line either. No burst pipe, loss or danger.

## Notebook

## Reveal and dread

The pump takes up with a heavy, satisfying thump and the gate gauge (seen later at R11) reads powered. Then one belt winds down and stops. The diaphragm does not. Its next inhalation pulls fine dust horizontally across the floor toward it (`motion`; still: dust streaks). The plate under the belt reads REFERENCE RESPIRATION.
